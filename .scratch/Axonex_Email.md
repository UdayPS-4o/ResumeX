## Notes on choices made

- **No salary, no availability.** The LinkedIn post asked for a CV. Naming a number first sets your own ceiling before anyone has assessed you, and "available immediately" reads as leverage-free. Both are answers to give when asked, in a conversation.
- **Relocation stays.** You're in India, the role is in Hong Kong — it's the first thing a reviewer wonders, and leaving it unaddressed makes them guess. One clause, stated as fact.
- **Opens with system ownership, not the app.** The title is Full Stack *Web* Developer; leading with "app on both stores" invites HR to file you as a React Native dev. "Database to browser to store listing" claims the full stack in their own vocabulary and lets the companion app land as evidence instead of identity.
- **"Three years" is deliberate.** It answers the 2–5 year requirement before anyone counts dates against a 2023–2027 B.Tech.
- **The email points at the CV instead of reciting it.** Three numbers, one AI sentence, then an explicit handoff to the attachment. Everything else earns its place in the document.
- **No claim here that isn't on the résumé.** Every figure — 9,000+, 100k+, 6,000+ — is one you gave me and one the document backs up.
