About Axonex
Axonex is an innovative technology company specializing in robotics and AI solutions, delivering intelligent products across security patrol, education, and companion domains. With a strong technical team and established product portfolio, we are expanding into the mobile internet space to develop cutting-edge family-focused companion applications.
As part of our business growth, we are looking for a talented and passionate Full Stack Web Developer to join our expanding team and help build next-generation digital platforms that integrate AI, robotics, and intelligent companion technologies.

Key Responsibilities
- Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable web applications from frontend to backend.
- Collaborate closely with product managers, UI/UX designers, mobile developers, and AI engineers to deliver high-quality solutions.
- Develop responsive, user-friendly, and high-performance web interfaces.
- Build and maintain secure, reliable, and scalable backend services, APIs, and databases.
- Integrate third-party services, cloud platforms, and AI-powered features into web applications.
- Optimize application performance, reliability, and security.
- Participate in technical design, code reviews, debugging, and troubleshooting activities.
- Support deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement of production systems.
- Stay current with emerging technologies and industry best practices in web development and AI-driven applications.
